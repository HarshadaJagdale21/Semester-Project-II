In today’s digital age, the need for secure password storage has become more critical than ever before. Numerous studies have highlighted that password-related security breaches are a major concern in cybersecurity. According to a recent report by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST, 2023), nearly 80% of hacking incidents occur due to weak or reused passwords. This statistic strongly emphasizes the importance of using password management tools to enhance online safety.

Research conducted by Springer in 2022 further supports this, revealing that the use of password managers can improve users' online security behavior by 60%. These tools not only help in generating strong and unique passwords but also assist in securely storing and managing them, reducing the chances of human error and password reuse.

Among existing password management solutions, LastPass is one of the popular tools; however, it is a proprietary system that requires a subscription and does not offer transparency in its internal mechanisms. On the other hand, Bitwarden is an open-source platform, which makes it flexible and accessible, but it lacks support for offline usage, which can be a limitation for some users.

To address these limitations, our proposed solution is designed using open and free technologies such as PHP, MySQL, and JavaScript. It is fully customizable, cost-effective, and accessible to users who need a simple yet powerful password management system. This approach not only ensures better control over user data but also promotes adaptability and ease of use.
